# Final Clearance: promo clips

Short gameplay clips for **Final Clearance**, an air traffic control game for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6821498143).

- `clips/`: vertical 1080×1920 videos with narration, original music and sound effects.
- `queue.json`: the posting schedule for the YouTube channel [@FinalClearanceGame](https://www.youtube.com/@FinalClearanceGame). A daily task uploads the next clip and records its video ID here.

All music and sound effects are original and synthesized for these clips.
